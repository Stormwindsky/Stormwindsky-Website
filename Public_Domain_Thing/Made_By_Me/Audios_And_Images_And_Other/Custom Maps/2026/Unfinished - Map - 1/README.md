## Installation Instructions

### 1. Official Vanilla Launcher (Windows & macOS)
1. Download the map folder.
2. Open your Minecraft launcher and ensure your game version is set to 1.20.1.
3. Open your saves directory:
   * **Windows:** Press `Win + R`, type `%appdata%\.minecraft\saves`, and press Enter.
   * **macOS:** Open Finder, press `Cmd + Shift + G`, type `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves`, and press Enter.
4. Extract and place the map folder directly into this `saves` directory.
5. Launch Minecraft, open Singleplayer, and select your new flatworld map.

### 2. Linux (Debian / Ubuntu and Derivatives)
1. Download the map folder.
2. Open your file manager or terminal. By default, Minecraft saves are located in your home directory at `~/.minecraft/saves`.
   * *Note:* If you cannot see the `.minecraft` folder, press `Ctrl + H` in your file manager to show hidden files.
3. Extract and place the map folder directly into the `~/.minecraft/saves` directory.
4. Open your Minecraft launcher, ensure your profile is set to version 1.20.1, launch the game, open Singleplayer, and select the map.

### 3. Prism Launcher (Cross-Platform / Linux, Windows, macOS)
1. Download the map folder (keep it zipped or extract it).
2. Open Prism Launcher and select your Minecraft 1.20.1 instance.
3. Right-click your instance (or click **Manage** on the right-hand panel).
4. Go to the **Folder** tab on the left menu and click on **Minecraft Folder** to open the instance directory.
5. Open the `saves` folder inside that directory.
6. Place the map folder directly into the `saves` folder.
7. Launch the instance from Prism Launcher, open Singleplayer, and select your new flatworld map.
